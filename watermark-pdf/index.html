Watermark PDF

Add a text watermark such as DRAFT, CONFIDENTIAL or your company name to every page of a PDF. Choose diagonal, horizontal or tiled placement, a color and how see-through it is. The watermark is added in your browser, so sensitive documents never leave your device.

Your files never leave your device. Everything runs in your browser: no uploads, no sign-up, no watermarks, works offline.

How to use Watermark PDF

  1. Drop a PDF into the box, or tap “Choose a PDF file”.
  2. Type the watermark text, e.g. CONFIDENTIAL.
  3. Pick the layout, color and opacity.
  4. Press “Add watermark” and download the watermarked PDF.

How it works

Can the watermark be removed?

It is drawn into each page, so it shows in every PDF viewer and when printed. Someone with a PDF editor could still delete it, so for strong protection also share the file only with people you trust.

Which characters can I use?

Latin letters, numbers and common symbols, using the standard Helvetica font built into every PDF reader. Emoji and non-Latin scripts are not supported yet.

Will my text still be selectable?

Yes. Your pages are not re-rendered; the watermark is added on top, so the original text, links and images stay exactly as they were.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. neverupload has no server that receives files. Everything happens inside your browser on your own device, and the files never leave it. You can even disconnect from the internet and the tool keeps working.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After your first visit the app is cached by your browser, so you can use it without a connection. You can also install it as an app from your browser menu.
