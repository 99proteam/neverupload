JPG to PNG

Change JPG images into PNG files in your browser. Add a single picture or a whole folder, adjust the quality, and download the converted PNG images (as a zip for batches). Nothing is uploaded: the conversion happens on your own device.

Your files never leave your device. Everything runs in your browser: no uploads, no sign-up, no watermarks, works offline.

How to convert JPG to PNG

  1. Drop one or more images into the box.
  2. Choose the format to convert to: JPG, PNG or WebP.
  3. Set the quality (and background colour for JPG).
  4. Press “Convert” and download your files.

How it works

What happens to transparency when converting to JPG?

JPG cannot store transparency, so transparent areas are filled with the background colour you choose (white by default).

Why can’t I create WebP files?

A few older browsers (mainly older Safari) cannot encode WebP. If that happens you will see a clear message; try another browser.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. neverupload has no server that receives files. Everything happens inside your browser on your own device, and the files never leave it. You can even disconnect from the internet and the tool keeps working.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After your first visit the app is cached by your browser, so you can use it without a connection. You can also install it as an app from your browser menu.
