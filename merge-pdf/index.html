Merge PDF

Merge PDF files online for free without uploading them anywhere. neverupload joins two or more PDF documents into a single PDF right in your browser, keeps every page exactly as it was, and lets you choose the order by dragging. No sign-up, no watermark and no file size limit.

Your files never leave your device. Everything runs in your browser: no uploads, no sign-up, no watermarks, works offline.

How to use Merge PDF

  1. Drop your PDF files into the box, or tap “Choose PDF files”.
  2. Drag the files (or use the arrow buttons) to put them in the order you want.
  3. Press “Merge PDFs”.
  4. Download your combined PDF.

How it works

How do I merge PDF files?

Drop two or more PDFs into the box (or pick them with the button), drag them into the order you want, and press "Merge PDFs". Your combined file downloads straight away.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. neverupload has no server that receives files. Everything happens inside your browser on your own device, and the files never leave it. You can even disconnect from the internet and the tool keeps working.

Is there a file size or page limit?

There is no fixed limit. Files of 100 MB and more work fine on most computers; the only real limit is your device’s memory.

Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

Not yet. Remove the password in your PDF reader first, then merge.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After your first visit the app is cached by your browser, so you can use it without a connection. You can also install it as an app from your browser menu.
