Add Page Numbers

Add page numbers to a PDF in seconds. Put them at the bottom or top, on the left, center or right, choose a style such as “Page 3 of 12”, start from any number and skip the cover page. Numbering happens in your browser, so contracts, theses and reports stay private.

Your files never leave your device. Everything runs in your browser: no uploads, no sign-up, no watermarks, works offline.

How to use Add Page Numbers

  1. Drop a PDF into the box, or tap “Choose a PDF file”.
  2. Pick where the numbers go and how they look.
  3. Optionally set the starting number or skip the first page.
  4. Press “Add page numbers” and download the numbered PDF.

How it works

Can I leave the cover page without a number?

Yes. Tick “Skip the first page” and numbering starts on page two. Combine it with “Start at” to begin from any number you like.

Does it work with rotated or landscape pages?

Yes. The number is placed relative to the page as you see it, so it stays upright and in the corner you picked on portrait, landscape and rotated pages.

Is the rest of my PDF changed?

No. Text stays selectable and links, bookmarks and images are kept. Only the page number text is added on top of each page.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. neverupload has no server that receives files. Everything happens inside your browser on your own device, and the files never leave it. You can even disconnect from the internet and the tool keeps working.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After your first visit the app is cached by your browser, so you can use it without a connection. You can also install it as an app from your browser menu.
